**Key teaching moments for the AI scorer:**
1. **Hitting the structure beats the seller's resistance** — Trial Close 1 + back-office anchor + novation pivot got the deal where Call #01's relational style left $30k+ on the table.
2. **Off-script promises are scoreable defects, not bonuses.** When a rep invents new services mid-call, that's a -2 to -3 to the relevant step, even if the seller likes it. The company may not be able to deliver.
3. **Wife/spouse anxiety calls for mechanics, not assurance.** "Don't worry, we're family" scores worse than "Totally fair concern — let me walk you through how the closing date gets set, the contingency we put in for finding your next house, and the amendment options if your timeline changes."
